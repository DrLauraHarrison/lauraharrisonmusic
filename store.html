A Note from the Shopkeeper...

Hello! Welcome to the future hub of all things musical, educational, and slightly chaotic. While I am currently hidden away in my studio putting the final, meticulous updates on my upcoming textbooks, this page is undergoing a beautiful transformation of its own.

I am moving out of my old digital storefront, and once the dust settles from the big move, this space will be fully stocked with everything my inner multi-passionate artist loves to create.
Here is a sneak peek of what’s coming to the shelves soon:

  • The Textbooks: My flagship music theory book, Enjoying Music Theory, alongside its partner in crime, Enjoying Music Improvisation.
  • Choral Arrangements & Transcriptions: Tailor-made charts for vocal ensembles built from decades of conducting and directing experience.
  • Solo Arrangements & Original Compositions: Lead Sheets of my original compositions or any solo arrangements I've made of standards. 
  • The Masterclass Series: Companion vocal technique pedagogical references and video resources to help you sing with total freedom.
  • Future Sounds: A front-row seat to help bring my upcoming live double album project, Bach and Bossa, to life!
