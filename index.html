Welcome to My Digital Studio!

Hello & Welcome!

I am so glad you stumbled into my corner of the internet. Whether you are a student looking to demystify complex chords, a choral conductor seeking fresh vocal arrangements, a fellow musician looking for some jazz inspiration, or an arts lover looking for a soundtrack to your day—you are in exactly the right place.

For over three decades, my life has been a joyful, dual-path journey split between the quiet focus of the writing desk and the vibrant energy of the stage. This website is my brand-new hand-built space to bring those two worlds together under one roof (and finally say goodbye to my old digital landlord!).

Where would you like to explore first?

  • The Author's Desk: Step into my writing studio. Here, you will find updates on my upcoming textbook, Enjoying Music Theory (2nd Edition), my work on Enjoying Music Improvisation, and my upcoming vocal technique masterclass video series.
  • The Performance Hub: Come hear the music! Dive into my dedicated genre spaces to explore my dual musical hearts—from the serene, historical reverence of early classical music to the fiery, liberating freedom of vocal jazz performance and composition.
  • The Shop: Sneak a peek at what's coming soon to the shelves, including solo sheet music, choral and solo arrangements, transcriptions, and upcoming fundraising details for my live double album project, Bach and Bossa.

Thank you for visiting, for your curiosity, and for sharing a love of music with me. Grab a warm drink, make yourself comfortable, and feel free to explore!

With kindness and joy,
Laura
